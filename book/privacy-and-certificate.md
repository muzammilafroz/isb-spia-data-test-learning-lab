# Privacy and certificate

The browser tests use local JavaScript only. Your answers, attempt history, progress, and certificate name are stored in `localStorage` on your device.

- Nothing is sent to a server.
- Clearing browser storage removes progress unless you export it first.
- Answer keys are shipped to the browser and can be inspected. The tests are for self-study, not secure examination.
- The completion record is self-assessed and cannot be externally verified.

The certificate becomes available only after all five modules and the capstone reach 80 percent. It is not an institutional credential.
